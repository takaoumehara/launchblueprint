# Brand, media and generated images

Generic lessons for brand identity, product films, captions and AI-generated images, learned across several teams (2026-11-04 batch; no product, client or person names). Each item maps one-to-one to `catalog/brand_media.yaml` (same id) with the **principle**, what to *avoid*, *why*, a yes/no *check*, and how many submissions reported it. Facts (style-guide limits, tool defaults, costs) are in `claims[]` with source and `verified`.

Related: rule `R-009` (captions as separate files), `catalog/ui_ux.yaml` (motion, autoplay), `reference/ui-ux.md`.

## Before visuals (idea)

- **bm.look-before-code**: For brand work, write the concept (insight, why only this brand, the mark system) and get the look and mood signed off by a creative reviewer before any visual round or site code; then show two or three finished options. *Avoid:* Opening a site PR in week one, or showing twenty quick variations to a decision-maker. *Why:* Volume without a concept read as amateur and cost a review round; site code was parked when the look changed. *Check:* Was a written concept and look direction approved before the first visual was shown and the first site commit? *Seen in:* 2 submissions.
- **bm.logo-mark-craft**: Draw the mark itself with a vector tool plus manual optical correction (or a designer); outline all text in logo SVGs; test the mark at 16 px, on dark icon backgrounds and for unintended readings. *Avoid:* A code-drawn SVG wordmark with live <text> and a web font. *Why:* Code-drawn marks lacked kerning and weight balance, the logo rendered in a fallback font as an image, and small marks read as other symbols. *Check:* Does the logo SVG contain no <text>, and was the mark checked at 16 px and for unintended readings? *Seen in:* 2 submissions.
- **bm.apparel-brand-sections**: An apparel brand blueprint has these sections before visuals start: origin and values, allowed moods on top of fixed rules, garment rules, series and wave plan (first capsule of about 6-10 pieces), inclusive casting, palette, image-generation rules, competitor research, the manufacturing path, no-fake-claims rules (generated images never shown as real stock), and the site and social look. *Avoid:* Discovering these as course corrections one by one. *Why:* Each section came up as a correction during the work, not in the plan. *Check:* Does the brand blueprint have every section before visuals start? *Seen in:* 1 submission.

## Production and delivery (launch)

- **bm.captions-separate-and-validated**: Deliver a clean video plus one SRT/VTT caption file per language (player track off by default), and validate every caption file with a script before any review: no overlaps, at most two lines, line length within the language's limit, minimum cue duration. *Avoid:* Burning captions into the picture, or shipping hand-timed cues unchecked. *Why:* Every language change or fix needed a re-render, and overlapping or long cues were sent back (rule R-009, now seen in six teams). *Check:* Is there a caption-free master plus a validated caption file per language? *Seen in:* 6 submissions.
- **bm.genimage-qa-gate**: Treat brief constraints (banned construction details, no clichés, one place and time of day, house style) as a human visual gate on every generated image, and run a cheap single-pose or single-prop style test before committing a shot list; keep a local vector fallback. *Avoid:* Trusting the prompt text: on-model images that only passed a prompt check, city landmarks returned despite instructions, chapters generated separately so the landscape changes. *Why:* The same defect failed three rounds in a row, and off-style poses and drifting locations forced regeneration. *Check:* Did a person check every shown image against each banned detail, and did each new pose or prop pass a style test first? *Seen in:* 4 submissions.
- **bm.safe-area-per-ratio**: Design text-safe areas separately for each aspect ratio (4:5, 9:16, 16:9) and check a contact sheet of every ratio for clipped text. *Avoid:* Reusing a composition made for one ratio in another. *Why:* Result text was cut off in 4:5, and the bottom of 9:16 is covered by app interfaces. *Check:* Did a contact sheet of every ratio show zero clipped text? *Seen in:* 2 submissions.
- **bm.render-qc-every-file**: Measure every rendered file, not just the first: integrated loudness and loudness range, colour tags (primaries/transfer), and output identical on another machine (fonts bundled, temp files unique). *Avoid:* Assuming a shared mix chain or export script gives the same result for every film. *Why:* One film came out with LRA 19.4, untagged colour shifted on players, and system-font scripts broke on another machine. *Check:* Does every render pass the loudness target, carry colour tags (e.g. bt709) and match when rendered elsewhere? *Seen in:* 2 submissions.
- **bm.recordings-legible-on-phone**: Record site screens for zoom and crop (smaller viewport, device scale factor 2) and check legibility at real phone size. *Avoid:* Shrinking a full desktop-width recording. *Why:* Text in the film could not be read on phones. *Check:* Is body text readable when the film plays at real phone size? *Seen in:* 1 submission.
- **bm.seamless-loop-identical-frame**: Build a loop so its last frame is its first (a hard cut on an identical frame) and measure the seam. *Avoid:* Crossfading the tail into the head. *Why:* The crossfade showed two poses at once and failed technical review. *Check:* Is SSIM between the last and first frame 0.99 or higher? *Seen in:* 1 submission.
- **bm.localize-on-screen-text**: A localized film or page translates the text visible on screen (UI captures, titles), not only the captions. *Avoid:* Translating the subtitles and leaving a screen in the original language. *Why:* One scene of a translated film kept English body text, and the release waited for the owner's decision. *Check:* In every scene of the localized version, is the on-screen text translated? *Seen in:* 1 submission.
