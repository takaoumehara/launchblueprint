# Blueprint intro — narration script (English)

Length: 86 seconds of picture, 181 spoken words, five beats.
Voice: calm, warm, unhurried (about 150 words a minute). Plain words; a middle-school reader should follow every line.
Picture is silent. Times below are when each line should be spoken so it lands on the matching shot.
The same lines are in `captions.en.vtt` (subtitles) and `timing.json` (for muxing).

---

## Beat 1 — Pain (0:00–0:16)

> AI can build a screen in minutes.
> Then, six weeks in, a store rule says your checkout must change.
> Money, rules, what depends on what: none of it was visible.
> I wish I'd known.
> Now you start over.

*Delivery:* light and confident on the first line, then slow down. Let "I wish I'd known." sit, quietly, like a thought said to yourself. Small pause before "Now you start over."

## Beat 2 — What Blueprint is (0:16–0:29)

> Blueprint is a map of your whole project, before you build.
> Every decision sits in its column: brand, screens, tech, money, rules, launch.
> One look, and you see it all.

*Delivery:* the turn. Brighter. Name the six columns evenly; each one lights up on screen as it is said.

## Beat 3 — How it works (0:29–0:45)

> Tap a decision. You get the options, one recommended pick, and the reason why.
> Compare it with the alternative we set aside.
> See what else changes if you switch.
> When it looks right, you approve it.

*Delivery:* easy and practical, like showing a friend. The taps on screen happen at "Tap", "Compare" and "approve".

## Beat 4 — Features (0:45–1:12)

> Add categories as your project grows.
> Lines show real dependencies, so you see what breaks before it breaks.
> Lessons from real projects return as foresight: surprises others already hit.
> AI proposes. Nothing is final until a person says yes.
> And AI coding tools like Claude Code, Cursor and Codex read the same map,
> through a local MCP server or a YAML export.

*Delivery:* steady rhythm, one idea per line. Give "Nothing is final until a person says yes." a little weight.

## Beat 5 — Close (1:12–1:26)

> See the whole project before you build.
> Join the early prototype.
> Creativity Is Everywhere, LLC.

*Delivery:* the first line is the line people should remember: slow, sure, no upward lift. Then a short pause, the invitation, and the company name in a lower, simple voice. Picture holds about four seconds after the last word.
